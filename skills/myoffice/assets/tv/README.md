# TV clips

The TV in the sofa corner plays these animated GIFs when the TV folder
(`--tv-dir`, default `~/.whatsmyagentdoing/tv`) has none of its own. All four are
in the public domain; sources were checked on Wikimedia Commons on 2026-10-31.

- `muybridge-race-horse.gif`: Eadweard Muybridge (died 1904), *Animal Locomotion*
  plate 626, the mare "Annie G." galloping, published 1887. Public domain (author
  died more than 100 years ago). Unchanged from
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Muybridge_race_horse_animated.gif
- `muybridge-buffalo.gif`: Eadweard Muybridge, a bison galloping, *Animal
  Locomotion*, 1887. Public domain (author died more than 100 years ago). Unchanged
  from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Muybridge_Buffalo_galloping.gif
- `apollo-launch.gif`: NASA, an Apollo launch (8, 11 or 12) from 16mm launch-tower
  film, 1968–69 (https://archive.org/details/APOLLO16MMLAUNCHVIEWS). Public domain
  (work of the US federal government). From
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:AnimApolloLaunch,16mm.gif, scaled to
  240 px wide.
- `apollo16-moon-salute.gif`: NASA, Apollo 16 EVA television: John Young's jumping
  salute by the flag, April 1972. Public domain (work of the US federal
  government). From
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Youtubeastronautsonmoonot3.gif, scaled to
  240 px wide.
